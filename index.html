Founded and envisioned by Uwase Jolie in Kampala, Uganda, Velvet & Thread was born out of a passion for timeless elegance, sustainable craftsmanship, and tactile luxury.

We partner directly with certified ethical mills to select 100% traceable merino wool and organic cashmere. Each piece is designed to withstand cold weather with a sophisticated Scandinavian aesthetic, blending warmth with sustainable luxury that lasts for generations.

Uwase Jolie

Founder & Lead Designer — Kampala, Uganda
